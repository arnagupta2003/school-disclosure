Our School & Heritage

A legacy of learning and opportunity for girls

Established in 1948, Vidya Bhawan Girls’ Senior Secondary School is a CBSE-affiliated, aided school run by the Vidya Bhawan Girls School Delhi society. Located in New Rajinder Nagar, the school serves girls through an education focused on academic growth, character development, and leadership.

Students in classes XI and XII can choose from Commerce, Arts, and Science. Across the school, learning is supported by a library, specialist laboratories, and spaces for sports and other activities.
