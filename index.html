Our School & Heritage

A legacy of learning and opportunity for girls

Established in 1948, Vidya Bhawan Girls’ Senior Secondary School is a CBSE-affiliated, aided school run by the Vidya Bhawan Girls School Delhi society. Located in New Rajinder Nagar, the school serves girls through an education focused on academic growth, character development, and leadership.

Students in classes XI and XII can choose from Commerce, Arts, and Science. Across the school, learning is supported by a library, specialist laboratories, and spaces for sports and other activities.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Room to explore, create, and play

From hands-on learning to team sports, students have opportunities to build knowledge, confidence, and friendships.

Academic streams

Commerce, Arts, and Science streams are available in classes XI and XII.

Learning spaces

A school library and specialist Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, and Home Science labs support learning.

Sports

Students can take part in football, box cricket, badminton, table tennis, athletics, and other games.

School activities

Celebrations and co-curricular activities bring students together and make school life memorable.
